Assistant: The logs show two issues happening at once. Looking more carefully:

But the real root cause is in session.c at line 247 - there's a connection_ref count underflow. The pool exhaustion was a symptom of connections not being released properly.

User: What's the specific bug in session.c:247?

Assistant: The bug is a missing session_release() call in the cleanup path. When sessions are cleaned up, the reference count for the connection isn't being decremented, so the connection appears still in use. This causes the pool to slowly fill up until it hits 100% and starts rejecting new requests.
